# Round A results: frame_critique.py on the danchi renders

Generated by `run_roundA.py` (profile `realistic`). Status values: PASS / WARN / FAIL / NA. Each cell is `status value`; *better* says which image is closer to the target for that test (status first, then distance from the target band). The judgments used as acceptance checks are the team lead's and partly the user's, not ground truth.

## Summary

**Run:** 25 frames from danchi v1–v5 and damage-proto. The 14 comparable pairs and the
damage-proto micro panels were run with the `realistic` profile. The bays used hand-drawn wall
masks (`masks/`) as stand-ins for an ID AOV. Diagnostic sheets are in `sheets/` and
sensitivity checks in `results/sensitivity.md`. Raw output is in `results/all_results.json`.

**All four acceptance checks pass** (table below). The r5 pass condition also holds: v3
ranks above v4 on surface realism. v3's street wall has a median L\* of 91 and passes
saturation. v4's sits at 99, is clipped, and fails saturation.

**Most useful tests**
- **`value_range`:** clipping plus the brightest large surface's L\*. v4 has 47 % of the bay
  frame clipped and walls at L\* 99–100. v5 sits at 42–57.
- **`saturation_budget`:** the pale over-tint share uses the Tokyo base-colour limits, with
  partly clipped pixels kept as a lower bound. It gives v4 bay 34 % and v5 0 %, and the
  ordering holds for Munsell factors 5–8 (v4 24–37 %).
- **`camera`:** catches the aerial-over-bare-plane miniature (v5 09), a void backdrop (v1 07)
  and strong keystoning (v4 gable, 23°).
- **`streak_evenness`:** needs a wall mask. Fringe share is v4 0.71 and v3 0.73 against
  v5 0.34, and photos give about 0.35. This is stable under mask erosion and frame halves:
  v4 0.71–0.74, v3 0.70–0.89, v5 0.20–0.45.
- **`micro_uniformity`:** in map mode on the variance wall, 1.1 against 3.86. On real renders
  (r4_cmp_wall_close panels i/iii), 1.77 against 4.05.

**Weakest tests**
- **`saliency`** warns or fails on 20 of 25 frames. The peak count is unstable, and it is
  not calibrated.
- **`palette`** calls all 25 frames analogous or near-neutral, so it is info only. It is
  useful only with `--ref`.
- **`depth_separation`** is n/a on 16 frames and heuristic on the rest. It needs a depth AOV.
- **`repetition` (near-copy)** fails straight-on regular facades (v2 05, v3 04) and the 9-up
  sheet itself. It cannot tell designed rhythm from clones without instance IDs. It also
  never separated the v4 and v5 bays (0.18 against 0.09, both pass).
- **`value_grouping`** fails close-ups made of equal horizontal bands (corridors, golden
  pieces). This may be fair for a hero shot but is noise for a documentation view.

**Where the tests disagree with the stated judgments**
- **v5's street view** was judged the most photographic. It still warns on `squint` (0.66),
  `spectral_slope` (−0.65, too much fine energy) and `depth_separation`, where v4's street
  passes all three. Either v5's distant facades really are busy and lack haze, or these
  thresholds are wrong for estate views. Round B should decide.
- **`saliency`** prefers v4's bay (1 peak) to v5's (6 peaks).
- **Streak periodicity is weak everywhere** (0.10–0.20). The v4 fringe is not strictly
  periodic. What makes it read as a fringe is that most of its streaks hang from the coping.
- **Without a wall mask the streak test is unreliable.** The colour-cluster mask cuts the
  dark streaks out of the wall and once ranked v5 worse than v4. With the automatic mask it
  therefore reports info only.

**Limits**
- The inputs are 8-bit JPEGs: clipping is read at 250, and the grain band includes JPEG
  noise.
- FOV is assumed at 54°, which affects pitch.
- There are no photographs on disk, so `reference_match` was shown with v5 as a stand-in
  reference.
- The masks are hand-drawn.
